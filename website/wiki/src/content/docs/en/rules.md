## Preface

This server is an RP/PvP server. If you want to play here, you will come into contact with both. If you only want to play PvE, this is not the right server for you.

## General

1. This is a German-speaking server.
2. Being present on the server Discord is mandatory – use the same name there as for your in-game character.
3. We want to have fun together here with Life is Feudal. So when you act, ask yourself whether you would want to be treated that way yourself.
4. Follow the instructions of the admins.
5. Absences must be reported on Discord in the appropriate channel.
6. Claims without a notice of absence are deleted after 14 days.
7. Rule violations must be reported with proof (screenshots/video material).
8. Players in a bright white robe are on site for admin duties. They are neither to be attacked nor to be roleplayed at.

## Character creation

1. You may create exactly 1 character on the server.
2. The character name must fit the medieval setting.
   - 2.1 Well-known names from films, series, books or games must not be taken over 1:1.

## Starter pack

You get your starter pack from an NPC in the starting area by solving their quest. As part of the quest you can choose between 2 starter packs:

1. **Craftsman:** tool set (shovel, pickaxe, saw, sickle, hatchet, knife, wooden mallet, all at Q60), 10x tier 3 food (Q80)
2. **Adventurer:** light padded armour (Q30), war axe (Q30), simple bow + 31 arrows (Q30), 10x tier 3 food (Q80)

## Building rules

1. We have adjusted the sizes of the claims and the materials needed for building on the server. For certain monument levels there are additional **requirements:**
   - Level 1 (radius: 20 tiles): 1 player
   - Level 2 (radius: 30 tiles): 2 players
   - Level 3 (radius: 40 tiles): 3 players
   - Level 4 (radius: 55 tiles): 4 players
   - Level 5 (radius: 75 tiles): 6 players + territory capital
2. A distance of 100 tiles must be kept to neighbouring monuments (use the claim planner on the live map for this).
   - 2.1 This distance can be smaller if the claims involved agree on a smaller distance. The server team must be told about this by ticket.
3. After a restart, claims are always at their full size and cannot shrink. **The monument must still be fed with coins.** Otherwise all your buildings lose half of their durability.
4. Defensive structures are tied to claim levels:
   - Level 1: palisade
   - Level 2 & 3: palisade with a walkway
   - Level 4: stone walls
   - Level 5: castle walls

   A higher level may also build all the defences of the previous levels.
5. The number of workshops that may be built (smithy, kitchen, carpentry, apothecary, tailor and jeweller's workshop) depends on the claim level:
   - Level 1: 0 workshops
   - Level 2: 1 workshop
   - Level 3: 2 workshops
   - Level 4: 3 workshops
   - Level 5: 4 workshops
6. Trees, forests and tree farms must be planted realistically – no more than 2 trees may be planted next to each other. The tree script automatically removes all trees that were not planted in line with the rules.
7. Ditches and holes may only be dug in such a way that they can be left from every tile.

## Territories

Founding a territory must be requested by ticket.

1. **Conditions**
   1. The territory comprises at least 3 claims.
   2. The territory's capital must be at least claim level 3.
   3. The territory has at least 6 inhabitants spread over all claims.
2. **Advantages**
   1. The territory's capital may build one additional workshop.
   2. The territory's capital may upgrade to claim level 5 (if the minimum number of players is met).
   3. The territory's capital gets its coat of arms in-game as a decorative shield.

For more information on territories, see the [server concept](/en/concept/).

## RP rules

1. RP takes place in written form in the local chat.
2. RP is compulsory: anyone who is roleplayed at must react in a plausible way and also give the other person time to react.
3. We play in a medieval setting. Behave accordingly.
4. Power-RP is only allowed with mutual consent. Power-RP covers any RP action that forces an action on the other person and leaves no chance to react (for example *Konrad grabs Lothar and throws him to the ground*).

## PvP rules

<ol class="legal">
  <li><strong>General</strong>
    <ol>
      <li>PvP must be preceded by RP. (Exceptions: see point 4)</li>
      <li>Fleeing opens PvP immediately.</li>
      <li>Mounts may be killed in PvP actions as long as they are standing near the action.</li>
      <li>Defenders may use a beacon fire to call for reinforcements. Lighting this beacon fire automatically opens the fight.</li>
      <li>Claim members may log in for fights in their own claim. If the fight takes place elsewhere, no reinforcements may log in for it.</li>
      <li>Anyone who swims in a fight wearing more than light leather or padded armour automatically loses it and has to surrender near the water.</li>
      <li>Players who are stuck must not be attacked.</li>
      <li>Players who surrender must not be attacked and may only get up when the winners tell them to. They may be knocked unconscious for looting (for example with a punch).</li>
      <li>Anyone who dies in a fight counts in RP as unconscious or badly injured and has to go to their grave to do RP.</li>
      <li>Incapacitated players may be looted completely.</li>
    </ol>
  </li>
  <li><strong>Raids</strong>
    <ol>
      <li>In a raid, the victim must always be given a way to resolve the situation without PvP.</li>
      <li>Raids are meant to bring tension into RP, not to primarily harm the people raided. Demands should be kept within a framework that is appropriate and realistic for the way the raiders act.</li>
    </ol>
  </li>
  <li><strong>Prisoners</strong>
    <ol>
      <li>Incapacitated players can in principle be taken prisoner.</li>
      <li>Prisoners must be roleplayed with by the claim where they are held, as long as they are online. If this does not happen, the prisoner automatically escapes. If the members of the claim where the prisoner is held are offline, the prisoner may return to their own claim for OOC activities in the meantime.</li>
      <li>Prisoners may in principle be held for at most 3 real-life days. Anything beyond that must be agreed.</li>
    </ol>
  </li>
  <li><strong>KoS (Kill on Sight)</strong>
    <ol>
      <li>KoS applies on the entire eastern island on which the castle ruin stands.
        <ol>
          <li>The bridges do not count.</li>
          <li>All players involved must be inside the KoS area.</li>
          <li>PvP that was started in the KoS area must not be continued outside of it.</li>
        </ol>
      </li>
      <li>At night (<strong>in-game time</strong> 18:35 – 01:05), KoS applies on the entire map outside of claims.
        <ol>
          <li>Anyone who has a torch equipped and is on a road must not be attacked under KoS.</li>
          <li>If someone escapes from a KoS situation into a claim, the KoS must not be continued. If someone then wants to continue the conflict, this must happen within the RP and general PvP rules.</li>
        </ol>
      </li>
      <li>Anyone who carries out KoS must be able to prove with a screenshot or video recording that the victim met the conditions for being attacked under KoS.</li>
      <li>Recognising attackers: during KoS actions, attackers and defenders in principle do not recognise each other.</li>
    </ol>
  </li>
  <li><strong>Sieges / overcoming defensive structures</strong>
    <ol>
      <li>Sieges must be registered by ticket by the attacker(s).</li>
      <li>Sieges must have taken place within 14 days after the ticket was opened.</li>
      <li>A claim may only be besieged every 14 days.</li>
      <li>Defensive structures may only be overcome with siege ladders or destroyed in a targeted way with siege equipment. During sieges, the ability to damage buildings is activated for the besieged claims. The central defensive building may likewise be damaged in a targeted way to gain access (see 5.8.2). Collateral damage to other buildings cannot be ruled out.</li>
      <li>During a siege, KoS applies to the parties involved, even inside the defending claim.</li>
      <li>Sieges last at most 2 real-life hours. Anything beyond that must be agreed.</li>
      <li>During a siege, players involved may rejoin the fighting right away even after an engine death.</li>
      <li>When is a siege over?
        <ol>
          <li>The 2 real-life hours of siege time have run out.</li>
          <li>The attacker has gained access to the central building that the defender named beforehand, and at least 1 attacker has got in.</li>
          <li>One of the two leading parties has given up.</li>
        </ol>
      </li>
      <li>What do the attackers get?
        <ol>
          <li>Two goals are recorded in the preceding ticket:
            <ol>
              <li>Loot (10 silver per claim member + 40 silver per workshop built)</li>
              <li>Vassalage / rebellion</li>
              <li>Freeing hostages / taking prisoners</li>
              <li>Other RP reason</li>
            </ol>
          </li>
        </ol>
      </li>
    </ol>
  </li>
</ol>
