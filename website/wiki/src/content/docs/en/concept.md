## The world

We play in the Wulpische Lande (the Wulpian Lands), on the island of Myralon, which lies in a strait between two continents (see also the [lore](/en/lore/)).

It is a fictional framework with developed regions, stretches of land, peoples and a backstory. In principle your characters may originally come from outside this world, and their origin may be inspired by our real world. However, regions outside the set framework are not common knowledge. So do not expect anyone to know about them.

## The island of Myralon

The island belongs to the Wulpische Lande, but it explicitly belongs to none of the three kingdoms of the continent (Nordmar, Mesyrna, Valesos). When the server starts, the island is in a state of resettlement after having been mostly uninhabited for a long time.

When you arrive, however, there are a few existing structures.

### The harbour town and the merchant

The merchant Lutero has had a settlement built on Myralon on his own initiative and had word carried to every corner of the Wulpische Lande that every baron, craftsman, landless knight or exile is welcome to seek their fortune here. Naturally, the whole thing would also be of benefit to him.

**Role of Lutero and his harbour town:**

- Buying player goods for the mainland
- Selling mainland goods to the players
- In roleplay, the only link to the continent
- Actor in a global quest series that is necessary to unlock certain game content, which can then be bought from Lutero at the trading post
- The place with the most NPCs, who will supply you with quests and rewards as well as lore information
- At the start of the server, some everyday items will also be for sale at Lutero's trading post (metalwork, food, woodwork and much more). Later, this range is meant to be taken over by player claims, and Lutero will then no longer have these goods shipped from the mainland.

### The castle ruin and the rogues

The castle ruin stands on the small partial island in the east of Myralon and is used again and again as a hideout, for shorter or longer periods, by rogues (mostly pirates).

**Role of the castle ruin:**

- a permanent KoS zone
- There will be NPC quests as well as events that require you to enter this island.
- There will be loot events where, for example, items left behind or hidden by rogues can be found.
- partly GM-played characters on site

## Conceptual mechanics

### Trade

- The skill cap of 800 points requires more trade between players.
- The offer of the server market is meant to be taken over, in a sensible way, by player claims through their own trading posts as soon as the claims can produce these goods themselves.
- Making many items requires a special workshop (smithy, kitchen, carpentry, apothecary, tailor, jeweller's workshop). Building these workshops will only be possible in limited numbers, so even larger groups depend on the help of other claims if they really want to use every area of the game. At the same time, we have tried to make each workshop attractive in its own way, so the choice of workshop should not be too easy.

### Preventing or delaying market saturation

- Food spoils over time.
- Weapons and tools can no longer be repaired, only maintained, and they lose maximum durability in the process.

### Workshops

**Smithy**

- gives a quality bonus on all smithing products
- is required to make wire, chain links, scales, small metal plates and metal plates for armour making

**Carpentry**

- gives a quality bonus on all carpentry products
- is required to make wooden shafts, straight rods and gate modules. Wooden shafts and straight rods are needed for various weapons.

**Kitchen**

- gives a quality bonus on all cooking products
- is required to make tier 4 and tier 5 food

**Apothecary**

- gives a quality bonus on all alchemical products
- doubles the amount of materials produced (for example, 2 flux herbs also give 2 flux here)

**Tailor**

- gives a quality bonus on all tailoring products
- is required to make heavy or fine padded armour as well as heavy or fine leather armour
- is required to make exclusive clothing and the masterly craftsman's clothing

**Jeweller's workshop**

- gives a quality bonus on all jewellery made
- is required to make rings and amulets with gemstones
- Rings and amulets with gemstones now give permanent attribute buffs to strength, agility, vitality and/or willpower for as long as they are equipped.

### PvP

- A permanent KoS zone as well as night KoS are meant to offer more possibilities for roleplay-loving players.
- New siege options (for example ladders and a siege tower) are meant to make sieges better to play and more attractive.
- Full permission to loot defeated players should make a victory more rewarding and, through the fear of losing what you carry, bring more tension to fights.
- **Balancing**
  - Armour no longer has buffs. This reduces the already absurdly high damage output of some weapons, and the choice of weapon should no longer automatically decide the choice of armour, or the other way round.
  - The attribute pool is enlarged by rings and amulets with gemstones.

### Territories

Territories are alliances of 3 or more claims that lay claim to an area on the island (for the exact conditions for founding one and the advantages, see the [rules](/en/rules/)).

Once a territory has been founded, there are 2 ways to expand it further:

1. **Diplomacy:** An agreement in roleplay with further claims (for example through a mutual treaty).
2. **Conquest:** A siege is registered by ticket, and one of the two stated goals is "vassalage". If the siege is then successful, the attacked claim is forced into the territory. (Important: conquest can only be used to expand an existing territory, not to found a new one.)

Once a claim is part of a territory, there are several ways to leave it again:

1. **Dissolution:** The territory's capital dissolves the territory.
2. **Diplomacy:** An agreement in roleplay that you want to detach your settlement from this territory (for example in return for a payment or future obligations).
3. **Rebellion:** The claim or claims stage a rebellion against the ruling claim in the form of a siege of the capital. The rebels can receive support from parties outside the territory.

### Quests and events

- There will be a global quest series in which all players can take part at the same time.
- There will be various NPC quests that are meant to give the world more depth as well as bring attractive rewards.
- There will be various events to boost the roleplay on the server.
