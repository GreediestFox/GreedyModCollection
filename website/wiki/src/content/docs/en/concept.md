## Structures on the island

### The merchant and the harbour

- Buying and selling goods from and for the mainland
- Roleplay figure for the global quest series
- The place with the most NPC quests

### The castle ruin

- PvP KoS zone (day and night)
- Certain quests send players there
- Loot events
- Some characters on site are played by GMs

## Changed and new game content

### Reducing and delaying market saturation

- Only one character is allowed per player.
- Food spoils.
- Armour can only be repaired with equipment maintenance at the workbench.
- Weapons and tools can no longer be repaired.
- The steel cost for Vostaskus is reduced by ⅔.
- The flux cost for steel is reduced to 10 per ingot.
- Workshops (smithy, kitchen, carpentry, apothecary) only become available later, and then only in limited numbers.
- Certain items can only be made at workshops, for example metal armour at the smithy, reinforced staves at the carpentry and 4- and 5-star meals at the kitchen.

### Global quest series

The quest series is announced on Discord and played by the merchant when needed. It motivates all players at once to pursue a certain goal in order to unlock certain progress, for example workshop tokens.

### NPC quests

- Craft master (harbour): skill points
- Harbour master (harbour): quest for the starter pack
- and much more

### Skill cap and day length

According to the community surveys:

- **Skill cap for crafting:** 800 points
- **In-game day length:** 5 real-life hours

## Claims

- Monuments must be fed with coins.
- Registered absence freezes the upkeep points.
- Claim levels have requirements and partly changed sizes. Defences and the number of allowed workshops depend on the claim level:

| Level | Radius (tiles) | Requirement | Defences | Workshops |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 20 | 1 player | Palisade | 0 |
| 2 | 30 | 2 players | Palisade with walkway | 1 |
| 3 | 40 | 3 players | Palisade with walkway | 1 |
| 4 | 55 | 4 players | Stone walls | 2 |
| 5 | 75 | 6 players + territory capital | Castle walls | 3 |

So that smaller claims have more room for design, the radius of the first four levels was increased by 5 tiles each.

## Workshops

Workshops are the smithy, kitchen, carpentry and apothecary.

- **Workshop tokens** are the items needed to build workshops. A limited number can be bought via the homepage, and more tokens are available from the merchant in roleplay.
- Consequently, not every claim will be able to build every workshop.
- **For solo players:** once the workshops have been unlocked through the global quest, workshops will open in the harbour town shortly afterwards. Solo players who do **not** belong to a territory can use them for a fee at an NPC. The right of use is time-limited but can be bought again and again.

## Territory

Anyone can found a territory on the island if the following is met:

- It includes at least **3 claims**.
- The territory capital is at least **claim level 3**.
- It has at least **6 inhabitants** spread across all claims.

Benefits:

- The territory capital may build one additional workshop.
- The territory capital gets its coat of arms in game as a hanging shield.

## KoS at night

In addition to the PvP zone (where KoS applies day and night), there is KoS at night on the server, with these restrictions:

- KoS applies only in the time window of **complete darkness**. The exact in-game times will be announced at server start.
- KoS is only allowed **outside claim areas**. It does not matter whether it is your own claim or someone else's.
- If someone escapes into their claim during the night, KoS is over for that situation. A KoS PvP action begun earlier must not be continued.
- Anyone **carrying a torch and** standing on a **road** must not be attacked under KoS.

:::tip[Important]
At night you can still be raided in a roleplay-compliant way on your own claim or on the road with a torch.
:::
