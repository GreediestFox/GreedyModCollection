# 04 - Custom workshops, crafting quality buff, garden time gate

## Idea (GreedyFox)
Port the MMO's workshop buildings (Jeweler's, Tailor's, Stonemason's, Ore Washer, Large Herbal Garden, Big Drying Frame, Big Tanning Tub, Oil Press, Woodshed, storage buildings) into the YO server so players can craft at the building like at the Carpenter's Workshop, give these workshops the vanilla crafting-quality bonus, and make the Large Herbal Garden work with a growth timer instead of instant duplication.

## What was done / tried
* Reverse engineering of the crafting pipeline to find where the vanilla quality bonus lives, and of the native "greenhouse" logic.
* A type alias for the Large Herbal Garden turned out to be blocked by the client (hard-coded window per type).
* Data-only crafting via repurposed, otherwise unused quest abilities proved to work; the economy of the first bulk recipes (5 herbs in, 20 out) was rejected as duplication, so a plant-then-collect time gate was built.
* A first version of the workshop buff was blocked by Windows Smart App Control until the owner disabled it.

## Output taken into the server
* Workshops with their own crafting windows: Jeweler's (ability 316 "Craft Jewelry"), Tailor's (existing ability 282), Stonemason's and Ore Washer (shared ability 64), Large Herbal Garden (ability 328 "Cultivate Herbs", recipes 5200-5265 + hidden collect recipes 5300-5365 + visible "Collect Herbs" 5299).
* Plus hooks `workshopBuff`, `herbGardenGate` and `greenhouseAlias` (see `docs/YO_SERVER_HOOKS.md` in the Plus contribution). Current config: x1.2 quality within 6 tiles of a Jeweler's (abilities 201, 316), Tailor's (88, 282) and Stonemason's (61, 62, 64) workshop; the garden takes 9 real hours (32400 s) per batch; the Ore Washer uses the same gate as an instant craft after a 60 s bar with a fixed quality curve and no workshop buff.
* Big Drying Frame and Big Tanning Tub keep their working state across restarts through the greenhouse alias (`behavesLike` 118 / 472, `finishedAsWorking`), recipes crafting 5 at once.

## Technical notes
* **Quality math** (both craft paths): `q = sum(material contributions) + SkillDepends/100 * skill`; the device path adds device influence and multiplies by 1.2 for six hard-coded workshop types (136, 139, 140, 141, 518, 519), 0.8 for a wrong device; then `trunc`, optional cap, `min(skillCap)`, `max(1)`. `craftWithTool` (double-click a held tool) has no buff at all. `checkExceptionalChance` is called by both paths just before the item is created and receives the crafter's **GameConnection** (not the Player) and a writable quality: that is the single best hook point. Player = `**(connection+0xB38)`; the tile is `Geo::WorldToGeoId(pos)`; "near a workshop" is decided by comparing with placed workshops polled from the database (terrain block = id/262144, row = (id%262144)/512, col = id%512).
* **Craft at a building (device-ability route):** the client and server each read their own `skill_types.xml`; an `<ability>` shows at a building when its `ent_req type="object_type_id"` lists the building. Device-craft ability classes (vtable slot 3 = 0x365BE0) are fixed per id, so a new workshop needs a free device-craft ability id. The craft window lists recipes whose `SkillTypeID` equals the skill row under which the *ability* is nested, so the ability has to be moved under the wanted skill row. Device recipes are matched by `recipe.StartingToolsID` = building type; the device requirement row (IsDevice) is native: its Influence feeds quality and its Quantity wears the building. Unused quest abilities (316 "Forge", 328 "Cook") are spare device-craft slots.
* Tub/drying recipes are chosen by `findRecipeForMaterial`, which ignores `StartingToolsID`; they are not craft-window recipes.
* **Greenhouse (vanilla Herbal Garden, type 1353):** needs exactly 1 item of category 666 (alchemy herb), exactly 10 of category 204 (water) and fewer than 7 boosters (bones 342, dung 1032, fish 340). Type 1353 is hard-coded in exactly two server places (`CmCraftworkManager::loadObjects`, and the use entry) and in **one client place** (`CmInventory::ShowComplexObjectInventory` picks the window by type id). A new type can therefore never show the greenhouse window without patching the client, which is why the Large garden uses the crafting route.
* **Time gate:** `craftWithDevice` decides plant / pending / collect from the craft context (`ctx[0]` connection, `ctx[4]` ability, `ctx[6]` target entity, recipe id = low 32 bits of `ctx[8]`, the garden id = 8 bytes at `entity+8`); `_finallyMakeItem` skips item creation while a plant craft is in progress. Batches persist in a text file (`garden recipe readyUnix`). Collect recipes use SkillDepends 100 so the herb quality follows the collector's skill (a value of 10 produced quality 7). Countdown texts are messages 5100-5152 (+5160 "nothing growing") in `cm_messages.xml` with `important="2"` so they show as on-screen hints.
* Vanilla growth time is 72 game hours; with `dayCycle` = 3 real hours per game day that is about 9 real hours. `dayCycle` also scales crop and tree growth and should not be changed casually.
* Pitfalls: a hook that searches the scene for nearby objects crashed the server in early experiments (use the database poll); a one-ability-per-(skill, building) rule applies; the economy balance lesson is to design conversions or caps, never flat multipliers.
